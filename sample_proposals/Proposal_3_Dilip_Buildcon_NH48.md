### 3. Structural Design & Material Engineering Specifications
Strict compliance with MORTH and IRC technical schedules:

1. **Concrete Mix Grades:**
   - **Grade M45:** Flyover deck slabs and precast PSC I-girders.
   - **Grade M35:** Substructure piers, abutment walls, and retaining structures.
   - **Grade M15:** Foundation leveling PCC.
2. **Reinforcement Steel:**
   - **Fe500D TMT reinforcement** bars sourced from Jindal Steel & Power Limited (JSPL) / Electrosteel.
3. **Pavement Composition:**
   - **Subgrade:** 500mm compacted earthwork (CBR $\ge 8.5\%$).
   - **Granular Sub-Base (GSB):** 200mm crushed gravel.
   - **Wet Mix Macadam (WMM):** 250mm plant mix.
   - **Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM):** 135mm binder course using VG-30 grade bitumen.
   - **Bituminous Concrete (BC):** 45mm wearing surface using VG-30 bitumen.
