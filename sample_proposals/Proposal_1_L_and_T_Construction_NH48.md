### 3. Structural Design & Material Engineering Specifications
All structural and highway pavement components strictly adhere to MORTH (5th Revision), IRC:112 (Code of Practice for Concrete Road Bridges), and IRC:37 (Guidelines for Design of Flexible Pavements).

1. **Concrete Mix Grades:**
   - **Grade M50:** High-Performance Concrete (HPC) for flyover precast box girders and bridge decks.
   - **Grade M40:** Controlled concrete for piers, abutments, pile caps, and vehicular underpasses.
   - **Grade M15:** Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) leveling course.
2. **Reinforcement Steel:**
   - High Yield Strength Deformed (HYSD) **Fe550D TMT rebar** exclusively sourced from primary producers (Tata Tiscon / JSW Neosteel) ensuring high ductility and earthquake resistance.
3. **Pavement Composition:**
   - **Subgrade:** 500mm compacted subgrade achieving minimum California Bearing Ratio (CBR) of 10%.
   - **Granular Sub-Base (GSB):** 200mm graded crushed stone.
   - **Wet Mix Macadam (WMM):** 250mm plant-mixed graded aggregates.
   - **Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM):** 150mm binder course with VG-40 bitumen.
   - **Bituminous Concrete (BC):** 50mm wearing course with Polymer Modified Bitumen (PMB-40) providing rut-resistant surface texture.
