### 3. Structural Design & Material Engineering Specifications
Exceeds mandatory MORTH and IRC:112 specifications for enhanced 100-year structural design life.

1. **Concrete Mix Grades:**
   - **Grade M50:** High-Performance Self-Compacting Concrete (SCC) for flyover box girders, bridge decks, and precast crash barriers.
   - **Grade M40:** Controlled concrete for substructure piers, pile foundations, and culverts.
   - **Grade M15:** Sub-base PCC leveling courses.
2. **Reinforcement Steel:**
   - **Fe550D TMT reinforcement** bars exclusively sourced from Tata Steel (Tata Tiscon 550D) guaranteeing high ductility and extreme seismic resistance conforming to IS 1786.
3. **Pavement Composition:**
   - **Subgrade:** 500mm stabilized subgrade with geogrid layer reinforcement (CBR $\ge 10\%$).
   - **Granular Sub-Base (GSB):** 200mm crushed stone.
   - **Wet Mix Macadam (WMM):** 250mm plant mix.
   - **Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM):** 150mm binder course using VG-40 bitumen.
   - **Bituminous Concrete (BC):** 50mm wearing course with Polymer-Modified Bitumen (PMB-70) providing superior fatigue resistance against heavy axle loads.
