### 3. Structural Design & Material Engineering Specifications
Design conforms fully to IRC, MORTH, and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications.

1. **Concrete Mix Grades:**
   - **Grade M45:** For flyover superstructures, PSC girders, and deck slabs.
   - **Grade M35:** For pier columns, pier caps, pile caps, and culvert structures.
   - **Grade M15:** Sub-base PCC leveling courses.
2. **Reinforcement Steel:**
   - **Fe500D TMT bars** procured from primary steel producers (JSW Steel / SAIL / Jindal) tested for high elongation and tensile strength.
3. **Pavement Composition:**
   - **Subgrade:** 500mm compacted soil layer (minimum CBR 9%).
   - **Granular Sub-Base (GSB):** 200mm grading II crushed stone.
   - **Wet Mix Macadam (WMM):** 250mm compacted in two layers.
   - **Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM):** 140mm binder course using VG-40 grade bitumen.
   - **Bituminous Concrete (BC):** 50mm wearing course with VG-30 paving bitumen.
